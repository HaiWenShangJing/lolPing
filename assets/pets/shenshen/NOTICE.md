# 深深 (Shenshen)

Frames cut from the transparent WebM clips of [MerZlin/dsh-pet-indesktop](https://github.com/MerZlin/dsh-pet-indesktop)
(`assets/characters/shenshen/videos`), which collects them from [PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet](https://github.com/PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet).
The character, the OC 「溟月」, is by the artist 上善无形, and the clips were made by community members.

This art is **not** covered by lolPing's MIT license. Its source publishes it under CC BY-NC-SA-like terms:
**personal, non-commercial use only**, with attribution and a link to the source kept. Do not use it in anything commercial.

`node tools/import-pets/import.mjs shenshen` regenerates these frames (needs ffmpeg with libvpx-vp9 and libwebp).
