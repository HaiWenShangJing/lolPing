# Doubao (豆包)

Generated by tools/import-pets/import.mjs and committed to lolPing's repository with the rest of assets/pets.

- The art is the sprite sheet of YChao18's Doubao desktop pet
  (https://github.com/YChao18/ChatGPT-Codex-Desktop-Pet-Doubao, commit b5ede4730426f8fafecf0f0770ce41fd894138b7), under the MIT License below.
  lolPing cut it into frames, a few of them mirrored or moved, and draws small hearts, "z"s and other marks over some;
  it did not draw the character.
- "Doubao" (豆包) and its character belong to ByteDance. This is an unofficial fan pet that ByteDance has not approved, and
  the MIT License below covers YChao18's work, not ByteDance's rights.

`node tools/import-pets/import.mjs doubao` downloads the sheet and regenerates these frames.
