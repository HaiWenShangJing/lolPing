# Doubao (豆包)

Generated by tools/import-pets/import.mjs and committed to lolPing's repository with the rest of assets/pets.

- The frames are drawn by tools/import-pets/doubao.mjs: an original short-haired girl in a purple and blue hoodie, not
  ByteDance's artwork. The official Doubao avatar is ByteDance's own brand image and is not included.
- Only the palette (the purple, blue and teal of Doubao's logo) is borrowed. "Doubao" and its logo belong to ByteDance;
  this is an unofficial fan pet.

`node tools/import-pets/import.mjs doubao` regenerates these frames.
